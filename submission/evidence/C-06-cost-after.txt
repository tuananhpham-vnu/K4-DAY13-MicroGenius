Setup: same as E-01 (Gemini, incident cost_spike ON), but with
COST_GUARD_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS=60 set before starting the server. GeminiLLM
passes this as max_output_tokens in google.genai.types.GenerateContentConfig,
hard-capping every response regardless of the fault-injection prompt suffix.
